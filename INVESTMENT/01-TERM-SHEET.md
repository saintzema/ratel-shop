### 3. Payment and completion

Payment in a single tranche on Completion, into the Company's corporate bank
account (not a personal account).

Completion is conditional on:
1. Board and, where required, shareholder approval of the allotment;
2. Execution of the Share Subscription Agreement;
3. Execution of an **IP Assignment** transferring all FairPrice intellectual
   property held personally by the Founder to the Company;
4. Delivery of the Company's up-to-date share register and CAC filings;
5. *(If the Investor is non-Nigerian)* Funds routed through an authorised dealer
   bank and a **Certificate of Capital Importation** issued to the Investor.

### 4. Class of shares — for decision

This term sheet assumes **ordinary shares**, which is the simplest structure and
gives the Investor the same rights as the Founder pro rata.

The parties should discuss whether the Investor instead requires **preference
shares** carrying a liquidation preference (typically 1x non-participating). At a
$55,000 valuation a preference is unusual and arguably unnecessary, but the
Investor may raise it. Take advice before agreeing to any multiple above 1x or to
a participating preference — both materially change founder outcomes on exit.

### 6. Board and governance

- The Board remains as currently constituted. The Investor is **not** granted a
  board seat at this investment size.
- The Investor is granted **observer rights**: the right to receive board papers
  and to attend board meetings without a vote. *(Delete if not agreed.)*
- The Founder continues as [[Chief Executive Officer]] and retains day-to-day
  operational control.

### 7. Investor information rights

The Company will provide the Investor:
- Unaudited management accounts **quarterly**, within 30 days of quarter end;
- Annual accounts within 90 days of financial year end;
- Reasonable notice of any event materially affecting the value of the Investor's
  shares.

### 8. Reserved matters

For as long as the Investor holds at least 5% of the issued share capital, the
following require the Investor's **prior written consent**:

1. Issuing new shares at a price below the price paid by the Investor;
2. Selling all or substantially all of the Company's assets or business;
3. Winding up, or entering into any merger or amalgamation;
4. Altering the rights attaching to the Investor's shares;
5. Any borrowing exceeding [[₦ THRESHOLD]];
6. Declaring a dividend;
7. Any transaction between the Company and the Founder or the Founder's
   connected persons, other than the Founder's agreed remuneration.

### 9. Founder undertakings

- **Vesting.** The Founder's shares are subject to [[4]]-year vesting with a
  [[1]]-year cliff, from [[DATE]]. *(An investor is entitled to ask for this; it
  protects them if the Founder leaves. It also protects the Founder against a
  co-founder departing early, should one join later.)*
- **Full-time commitment** to the Company.
- **IP assignment** (see clause 3.3) and ongoing assignment of IP created in the
  course of the business.
- **Non-compete** for the duration of shareholding plus [[12]] months.

### 10. Anti-dilution and pre-emption

- **Pre-emption:** the Investor has the right, but not the obligation, to
  participate pro rata in future issues of shares.
- **Anti-dilution:** [[none / broad-based weighted average]] — narrow-based
  ratchets are aggressive at this stage and should be resisted.

### 11. Transfer restrictions

- **Right of first refusal:** neither party may transfer shares without first
  offering them to the other on the same terms.
- **Tag-along:** if the Founder sells a controlling interest, the Investor may
  join on the same terms.
- **Drag-along:** if holders of [[75]]% or more accept a bona fide offer for the
  whole Company, remaining holders may be required to sell on the same terms.

### 12. Confidentiality *(binding)*

Each party keeps confidential the existence and terms of this term sheet and all
information disclosed in connection with it, save as required by law or to
professional advisers.

### 13. Exclusivity *(binding)*

The Company will not solicit or negotiate any competing investment for **[[30]]
days** from the date of this term sheet.

### 14. Governing law *(binding)*

The laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. The courts of [[Lagos / Anambra]]
State have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 15. Costs

Each party bears its own costs, whether or not the investment completes.
