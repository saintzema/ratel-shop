## 7. UNDERTAKINGS OF THE COMPANY AND FOUNDER

7.1 **Use of proceeds.** The Subscription Price will be applied to the growth of

7.2 **Information rights.** For so long as the Investor holds at least 5% of the

(b) sell all or substantially all of the Business or its assets;
(c) pass a resolution for winding up, or enter any merger or amalgamation;
(d) vary the rights attaching to the Subscription Shares;
(e) incur borrowing exceeding [[₦ THRESHOLD]] in aggregate;
(f) declare or pay any dividend;
(g) enter any transaction with the Founder or a connected person otherwise than

7.4 **Founder commitment.** The Founder will devote substantially the whole of his
working time to the Business and will not, while a shareholder and for [[12]]
months afterwards, engage in any business competing with the Business in Nigeria.

## 8. PRE-EMPTION AND TRANSFER

8.1 **Pre-emption on issue.** Before issuing new shares, the Company shall offer
the Investor the right to subscribe pro rata on the same terms.

8.2 **Right of first refusal.** No Party may transfer shares without first
offering them in writing to the other Parties on the same terms, who shall have
[[20]] Business Days to accept.

8.3 **Tag-along.** If the Founder proposes to transfer shares carrying more than
50% of voting rights, the Investor may require the buyer to purchase the
Investor's shares on the same terms.

8.4 **Drag-along.** If holders of [[75]]% or more of the issued share capital
accept a bona fide arm's length offer for the whole issued share capital, the
remaining holders shall transfer their shares on the same terms.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

business acquired under this Agreement, except as required by law or disclosed to
professional advisers under equivalent obligations. This clause survives
termination.
