## 1. THE INVESTMENT

**1.1** The Investor pays the Company **USD $5,500** (or its Naira equivalent at
the CBN rate on the payment date) (the **"Purchase Amount"**) on or about the date
of this Agreement.

**1.2** In exchange, the Investor receives the right to certain shares in the
Company, on the terms below. **This Agreement does not itself transfer shares.**

**1.3** Defined terms:

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| **Valuation Cap** | USD $[[150,000]] |
| **Discount Rate** | [[80]]% (i.e. a [[20]]% discount) |
| **Qualifying Financing** | A bona fide equity financing in which the Company raises at least USD $[[50,000]] in new money, at a price per share set in that round |
| **Liquidity Event** | A sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, a change of control, or a listing |
| **Dissolution Event** | Voluntary winding up, general assignment for creditors, or cessation of business |

## 2. CONVERSION

**2.1 On a Qualifying Financing.** The Purchase Amount converts automatically,
immediately before the closing of that financing, into shares of the same class
issued in that financing. The number of shares is the Purchase Amount divided by
the **Conversion Price**, being the **lower** of:

(a) the **Discount Price** — the price per share in the Qualifying Financing
multiplied by the Discount Rate; and

(b) the **Cap Price** — the Valuation Cap divided by the Company's fully diluted
capitalisation immediately before that financing.

*Worked example, illustrative only.* If the Qualifying Financing values the Company
at $500,000 and the Valuation Cap is $150,000, the Cap Price is far lower than the
Discount Price, so the Cap Price applies. The Investor's $5,500 converts as though
the Company were worth $150,000 — approximately **3.7%** — rather than the ~1.1%
the round price alone would give.

**2.2 On a Liquidity Event before conversion.** The Investor receives, at the
Investor's option, whichever is greater:

(a) the **Purchase Amount** returned in cash; or

(b) the shares the Purchase Amount would convert into at the Cap Price.

**2.3 On a Dissolution Event.** The Investor is entitled to the Purchase Amount,
payable ahead of any distribution to holders of ordinary shares, and ranking
equally with any other SAFE holders. **This does not make the Purchase Amount a
debt** — it is payable only out of assets available for distribution, and the
Investor has no right to demand repayment at any other time.

**2.4 No maturity.** This Agreement has **no maturity date and bears no interest.**
It is not a loan. It continues until it converts under 2.1, or terminates under
2.2 or 2.3.

Days** before completing a Qualifying Financing or a Liquidity Event.

**3.4** The Company will not, while this Agreement is outstanding, amend its
constitutional documents in a way that adversely and disproportionately affects
the rights of SAFE holders as a class.
